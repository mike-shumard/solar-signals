Who it's for

The best fit is a property where power from the utility is missing, expensive to bring in, or unreliable. That's where a kit pays for itself fastest.

No power lines

Off-grid land and cabins

When the utility wants tens of thousands to run a line out, a kit can cost less than the hookup on day one.

Many units

RV parks, glamping and lodges

Cabins, bathhouses, pumps and guest EV charging, without a generator running all night.

Remote loads

Ranches, farms and vineyards

Well pumps, gates, barns and lighting far from the nearest pole.

High rates

California homes

California pays some of the highest power prices in the country, so solar pays back much faster here than in Arizona or Nevada.
